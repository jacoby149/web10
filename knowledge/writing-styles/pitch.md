# Pitch Style

For outreach, decks, demo scripts, and creator-facing messaging.

## Rules

- One sentence hook. No more than 15 words.
- The problem before the product. Let them feel the gap first.
- Numbers that land: "1M subs, 300K shown" beats "poor reach."
- The ask is ADD, not MOVE. Frame it as one more surface, not a migration.
- End on ownership. The emotional payload is "this is yours."
- Never apologize for what the product doesn't do yet. Scope honestly, pitch confidently.

## Structure

1. The number that proves the problem
2. The wedge (why now)
3. The mechanism (how it works, in one sentence)
4. The ask (add, don't move)
5. The payoff (ownership)

## Voice

Conversational but sharp. Write like you're talking to a smart person who doesn't have time for fluff. Reference THE STORY in plan.txt for the master narrative.
