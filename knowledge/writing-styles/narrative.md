# Narrative Style

For plan.txt, manifesto, story docs, and anything that needs to persuade through coherence.

## Rules

- The thesis in the first three lines. No warmup.
- Each paragraph advances one beat. If it doesn't, cut it.
- Use concrete numbers, not "many" or "a lot."
- The fear must be real. The solution must be structural, not aspirational.
- No jargon without definition. If you coin a term, define it in the same breath.
- End with the ask. What should the reader do, think, or feel?

## Voice

Direct, urgent, unhedged. Write like you've lost sleep over this. The plan.txt is the reference — read it for cadence.
