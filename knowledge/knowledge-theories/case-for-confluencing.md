# The Case for Confluencing

Confluencing is the practice of bringing disparate knowledge sources together into a single, navigable whole.

## Why It Matters

1. **Discovery**: When knowledge is siloed, it's effectively lost. Confluencing makes it findable.
2. **Context**: Ideas gain meaning from what surrounds them. A flat list of docs loses the relationships between concepts.
3. **Compound value**: Each new piece of knowledge that links to existing knowledge multiplies the value of both.

## The Alternative

Without confluencing, teams accumulate "documentation debt" — files that exist but aren't read because no one knows they're there or how they connect.
