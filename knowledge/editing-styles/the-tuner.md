# The Tuner

Surface polish. The structure and content are right — now make it sing.

## When to Use

Final pass before shipping. Text that's structurally sound but reads flat. Public-facing content where voice matters.

## Rules

- Vary sentence length. Three long sentences in a row puts the reader to sleep. Follow a long sentence with a short one.
- Strong verbs over verb + adverb. "Ran" not "ran quickly." "Failed" not "failed completely."
- Active voice. "The API rejects the request" not "The request is rejected by the API."
- Remove filter words: "see," "hear," "feel," "notice," "realize." The reader is already experiencing it.
- Replace nominalizations with verbs: "make a decision" → "decide," "perform an analysis" → "analyze."
- Check rhythm. Read it aloud. Where you stumble, the reader will too.
- Consistent terminology. Pick one word for one thing. Don't swap "node" and "instance" and "server" interchangeably.

## Voice Pass

- Does it sound like a human wrote it? If it sounds like a committee, rewrite.
- Is the tone matched to the audience? Technical for engineers. Narrative for stakeholders. Pitch for prospects.
- Are there dead metaphors doing work? "Deep dive," "at the end of the day," "moving the goalpost" — kill them.

## The Test

Read it aloud once. If you didn't edit anything, you weren't listening hard enough.
