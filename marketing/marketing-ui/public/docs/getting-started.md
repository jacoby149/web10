# Getting Started

**Who this is for:** you — a new web10 user. No technical background needed.

## What web10 is

web10 is a social platform where **you own your data**. Your posts, your
followers, your account — they live on a node that you (or the people you
follow) can stand behind, and they're yours to take with you. The pitch in
one line: **your audience, no shadow ban.** When you post to your followers,
100% of them see it — that's how the platform is built, not a promise.

## Create your account

1. Open the web10 social app and tap **"Log in or create your account."**
2. A small **web10 authenticator** window opens — that's where accounts live.
3. Pick a **username** (lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens) and a
   **password**.
4. If the node asks for it, add a **phone number or an email** — you'll get a
   6-digit code to confirm it. Many nodes (including web10.app) require one,
   because a real, reachable person behind the account is the whole point.
5. Done. You're signed in.

The authenticator is the front door for everything account-related — signing
in, managing your data, approving apps. You'll come back to it often.

## Sign in

Next time, the same button opens the authenticator and you sign in one of two
ways:

- **Username + password** — the classic way.
- **Phone or email** — enter the number or address on your account, get a
   6-digit code, enter it, and pick your account if you have more than one on
   that contact. This is also how you reset a forgotten password — see
   [Account Recovery](/docs/account-recovery).

If you've signed in before on this device, you usually won't see the popup at
all — your session is restored automatically.

## Your first post

1. In the app, tap the compose button (the **+**).
2. Write something — text, a photo, a video.
3. Choose where it goes. The default is the **public board** — the place
   everyone on the node can see. You can also share to specific groups
   (your followers, a community, close friends) — see
   [Groups in Plain Terms](/docs/groups-in-plain-terms).
4. Post it.

Your post is **your data**. It lives on the node under your name, and you can
edit or delete it any time.

## Follow a creator

1. Open a creator's profile.
2. Tap **Follow**.

That's it — it's instant. You'll see their posts in your feed. Following
means you joined their audience: they can see you're a fan, and (if they
choose to) they can reach out to you directly. Unfollowing is just as
instant.

## Where things live

- **Your profile** — your posts, your name, your face. This is your page.
- **The feed** — posts from the people you follow, plus the public board.
- **The authenticator** — your account, your data controls, your apps. This
  is where you take charge (see [Your Data](/docs/your-data)).
