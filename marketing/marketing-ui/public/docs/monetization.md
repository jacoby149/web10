# Monetization

**Who this is for:** you — a creator with an audience on web10 who wants to
start earning from it, and is opening the ad maker for the first time. This
is the front door to the money docs: one breath on what monetization is
here, then a route to the doc that answers your next question. It points
into the other docs — it doesn't repeat them.

## The one rule

On web10, **an ad is a post with a link that pays.** Content you made —
your text, your photo, your video — carrying a link that pays you when
someone clicks. The money flows from the affiliate program straight to you;
the platform is the delivery, not the middleman. That's the whole model.
Everything else in these docs is a detail of that one rule.

## Keep two layers straight

The split that comes up in every doc here:

- **Your ads** — the offers you chose: an Amazon tag, a brand you DM'd, your
  own store. This is your money.
- **Node ads** — the node operator's inventory, attached at a percentage.
  Not your money, not your decision. It rides along; you don't manage it.
