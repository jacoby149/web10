Media

Upload an image to MinIO, store it in your document as a minio type, and read it back as a fresh presigned URL. Upload a video — pick a style (TikTok, Instagram, square, original) and it gets reframed in your browser, then transcoded to HLS on your node: adaptive bitrate, signed segments, any aspect ratio. The blob lives in the object store; your document only carries the reference.
