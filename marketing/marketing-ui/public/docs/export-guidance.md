# Export Guidance

Step-by-step guides for exporting your data from each platform. The exports
are ZIP files you keep — they're what you'll upload when you
[import to web10](/docs/import-from-other-platforms).

## Facebook

**Export page:** [Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/212802592074644)

### Steps

1. Log into Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy
2. Navigate to Your Information → Download Your Information
3. Select date range, media quality, and file format
4. Request a download and wait for Facebook to prepare your archive
5. Download the ZIP when ready

---

## Google (YouTube)

**Export page:** [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/settings/takeout)

### Steps

1. Open [takeout.google.com](https://takeout.google.com)
2. Deselect all, then check YouTube (and any other services you want)
3. Choose export frequency and file size
4. Create the export and wait for Google to prepare it
5. Download the ZIP file(s) when the notification arrives

---

## X (Twitter)

**Export page:** [X Help — Accessing Your X Data](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/accessing-your-x-data)

### Steps

1. Log into X and go to Settings and Support
2. Navigate to Your Account → Download an Archive of Your Data
3. Confirm your password and request the archive
4. Wait for X to email you when the archive is ready (up to 48 hours)
5. Download the archive from the email link

---

## Instagram

**Export page:** [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/181231772500920/)

### Steps

1. Open Instagram Settings → Your Activity → Download your information
2. Select the date range and media options
3. Choose format (JSON or HTML) and submit the request
4. Wait for Instagram to prepare your data (up to 48 hours)
5. Download the exported data when notified

---

## TikTok

**Export page:** [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7543597460594285112&category=web_account)

### Steps

1. Open TikTok and go to Profile → Menu → Settings and Privacy
2. Navigate to Account → Download your data
3. Select the data you want to export
4. Submit the request and wait for TikTok to prepare it
5. Download the exported data when ready

---

## web10

You can also export data from your current web10 node and import it on another node. Your data is yours — take it with you, no lock-in.
