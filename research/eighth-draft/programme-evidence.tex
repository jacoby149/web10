\subsubsection{Complete K20 reads and physical access paths}
\label{sec:ev-programme}

The later programme uses a different regime from the sequential fan-out
trial: a synthetic million-account input with 1.6 billion platform events,
an independent response oracle, and complete uniform K20 research reads.
Authorship and graph shape are modelled from observational work
\cite{vanmierlo,kwak,ugander}; those observations do not determine request
arrival rates. The generator adopts 85\% never-posting accounts as a
modelling choice, not as an observation about web10. The authorship study's
four communities yielded 63,990 accounts and 578,349 posts; the top 1\%,
next 9\%, and remaining 90\% authored weighted shares of 74.7\%, 24.0\%,
and 1.3\%. The \record{REALISM-RERUN-PLAN.md}{realism contract} separates
authorship, graph shape, population, and engagement.

\textbf{Precomputation is not placement.} In the standalone ordinary
comment cell, a named tally lookup read 1,600 rows and 107,200 logical
bytes instead of 247,081,302 rows and 17,060,447,628 bytes for read-time
aggregation. A separate complete optional-projection cell fell from
59.46\,GB to 224.52\,MB at exact response parity, but changed both when
counts were computed and where they were placed. The diagnostic is not
additive to that complete comparison. The
\record{evidence/engagement-tally-20261119/RESULTS.md}{tally receipt}
also reports an omitted paired verification domain; it did not invalidate
the ordinary observation or establish the missing paired result.

Exact distinct delivery counts merge reader sets across days rather than
sum daily distinct counts. Before day partitioning, 294 native daily
inserts finished but the \record{evidence/all-fixes-two-grain-20261120/RESULTS.md}{seed}
did not complete. On the day-partitioned store, all 319 daily inserts read
1.6 billion rows and reconciled exactly 1.6 billion events, excluding
layout construction and census costs. One-shot all-time merging hit the
memory cap; subsequent bounded merging completed in 489 batches
(\pr{1603}), preserving exact distinct semantics. These are build costs,
not complete-read costs. Later isolated composition gates passed with exact
responses while read budgets still failed; the first combined row run's
query-ID bridge did not produce bankable row measurements
(\record{evidence/all-fixes-reconciled-20261122/RESULTS.md}{composition receipt}).

\textbf{Order for the lookup.} A full-post-key billboard pin index cut
candidate discovery from 82,897,093 rows, 8.72\,GB, and 3,385\,ms native
elapsed to 32 rows, 2,912 bytes, and 6\,ms. The 32 rows are the entire
pin population of the fixture, not a production worst case. The same-store
complete logical-byte observation fell from 8.85\,GB to 123.12\,MB;
creative authority remained canonical
(\record{evidence/skinny-pin-20261122/RESULTS.md}{pin receipt}, \pr{1618}).

\textbf{Keep bodies as payloads.} All six isolated post-family assembly
cells passed exact key, type, and value parity, yet crossed the predeclared
tenfold rejection threshold on at least one measured axis. For ordinary
post-open, rows read rose from 8,192 to 1,817,510 and logical bytes from
1,293,268 to 155,195,815. Paired P feed assembly rose from 8,192 to 244,185
rows and from 169,943 to 21,591,973 bytes. A separate single fat-payload
control rose from one to 90,115 rows; it is not an engagement thread.
The conclusion is about maximal decomposition, not every narrower
row layout or complete-feed latency
(\record{evidence/row-model-4096-20261123/RESULTS.md}{six-cell receipt}).

\textbf{Transient fixed slice.} Deferring bodies from the key scans left
complete captured reads effectively unchanged: indexed 620.85\,MB versus
projected 622.46\,MB (6.014 versus 6.021\,s HTTP). Native plans still
selected 323 and 54 attachment-history granules. Replacing those reads
with a skinny layout ordered by document, group, version and deletion flag
cut the two scans to 21 and one granule. Both measurement orders matched
the independent oracle: indexed 620.85\,MB versus hybrid 182.34\,MB,
a 70.6\% drop. HTTP wall time rose from 5.742 to 6.739\,s and from
5.490 to 6.337\,s in the respective orders. Summed native duration was
1,094 versus 1,093\,ms and 1,054 versus 1,059\,ms; statement peaks were
roughly 86--91\,MB. Generation checks remained in the path, not a
separately isolated cause of the full wall-time increase. The frozen lab
passed seven named fault cases with 43 native receipts; the six-case live
publish/cleanup gate was not admitted
(\record{evidence/k20-projected-20261125/RESULTS.md}{projected receipt};
\record{evidence/k20-attachment-20261125/RESULTS.md}{hybrid slice receipt}).

\textbf{Post-merge full store.} On the retained frozen store the hybrid
satellite copied 92,201,626 link-history rows in 32 subdivisions. All
subdivisions had zero census mismatches and zero tombstone ties. Two
measured reads matched the independent ordered complete-response oracle.
Each read 1,424,566 rows and 92,998,217 logical bytes; HTTP times were
9.510 and 8.722\,s, summed native durations 1,533 and 1,390\,ms, and
largest statement memory 106.2 and 110.2\,MB. The cited indexed
observation was 123,119,689 logical bytes and 7.017\,s HTTP, not a
contemporaneous rerun. The post-merge byte ratio is 0.7553 (24.5\% lower),
not the transient slice's 0.294. The full-store fault suite and live
six-case comparison did not run
(\record{evidence/full-store-hybrid-20261126/RESULTS.md}{full-store receipt}, \pr{1679}).
