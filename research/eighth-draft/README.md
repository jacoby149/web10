reads, isolated build/assembly cells, and the historic production observation.

transfer. The [full-store hybrid receipt](../experiments/costfn/evidence/full-store-hybrid-20261126/RESULTS.md)
is a measured result; the [DAU receipt](../experiments/costfn/evidence/dau-sustain-20261127/RESULTS.md)
records a reader-authority preflight refusal, not calibration or a load result.

R630's disk array.
