a new read pattern is a new backend
So the read model comes one size: a new ranking or a new join means a new replicated backend, not a new query. And the model has no shape for a hi-score or a listing — you’d hack a post, or a custom lexicon outside the social graph. Delegation is app-to-user-data: a second app holds no typed read on the first app’s data; there is no shared store to query across.
