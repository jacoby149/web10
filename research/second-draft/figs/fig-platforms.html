delegation to your data, not interop between apps
An app gets a delegated token to your data — Mastodon’s coarse read / write scopes, Bluesky’s per-collection, per-action permissions. But it’s app-to-user-data on a fixed model: a high score or a listing has no shape in it (you’d hack a post — or a custom lexicon that sits outside the social graph), and no second app holds a typed read on the first app’s data. Federation replicates the model across servers; it doesn’t add apps, or compose across them.

A second app gets in through a per-service grant the user gives: the snake game holds a typed read on the social app’s profile — and nothing else (no grant → 403). The user owns these grants as data, and the apps compose across them on one store: OAuth, but app-to-app and fine-grained — a role and a policy, no protocol change.
