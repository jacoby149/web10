How money flows on web10
the ad architecture — service-agnostic documents, creator links, and a separately configured operator overlay
the surfaces — the generic rail is available; applications supply catalogs and renderers
the social app
the first app to build the catalog — for now, the only one.
its ads are posts: the creative + the offer, the link that pays
the snake game
eligible documents can use the same rail

the marketplace
same generic mechanism; app-owned catalog and renderer
not a measured advertising integration
the node — the ad rail: service-agnostic at the mechanism, app-owned at the catalog, node-controlled at the density
result preparation attaches readable creatives — an authorized post is not a blanket creative grant
• the creator’s layer: ad_preference (pinned) → doc.ad; proposed dense use on 80–90% of content, chosen by the creator — not measured exposure
• the node’s layer: configured density → doc.node_ad; proposed 5–10%, default 10%; deterministic per reader with inventory rotation
• both may accompany one post — the overlay does not replace the creator’s offer; the operator sets overlay density
the ad inventory in the store — no new tables, no new collection, no ad network
app-owned documents tagged ad — creator inventory  ·  eligible documents tagged ad + node_ad — public Discover inventory  ·  the offer: kind (affiliate · direct · own_store) · the link that pays · cta · disclosure — the platform never rewrites the link, never cloaks it
the money — off-platform: the node processes no payment (the ad pureplay)
the creator — keeps 100%
affiliate and sponsor payouts settle off-platform — their Amazon tag, their own store. The link is never rewritten or cloaked, there is no cut of ad revenue, and no payment is processed: the link pays the creator, the way it always did.
the node operator — a media company
can sell its own overlay inventory directly, off-platform, like a publication sells ad space. Operator inventory revenue, creator affiliate payouts, and hosting or platform fees are separate lines. Competition on overlay density is an intended incentive, not an observed market or measured revenue share.
the v4 horizon — a separate layer, not the v3 claim
brands buy inventory (the exchange: CPM / CPC / CPA, DSP/SSP) · the payment rail (memberships, tips, Stripe Connect) · the creator’s own ad layout. The v3 ad pureplay has no ad network, no programmatic bidding, no third-party targeting.
the policy — creator payouts stay with the creator; operator overlays remain separate
No percentage of creator affiliate or sponsor payouts is taken by this rail. Other protocols can support creator revenue too; this figure describes web10’s mechanism, not an exclusive monetization capability.
