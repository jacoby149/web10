# Craft — how it looks and sounds

Three files, each one a gate on what enters the manuscript:

| File | What it governs |
|---|---|
| `voice.md` | the 13 editorial rules — the no-hedging discipline, punchline-first, scope-as-fact, the awe beats as structure |
| `figures.md` | the look — the design language (the dark web10 theme, semantic color, the same mechanism drawn identically), the figure that maps the paper, and the accuracy invariant; the full per-figure rationale lives in `../../second-draft/kb/diagrams.md` |
| `evidence.md` | experiments as proof — the claim taxonomy (architectural / business / scale) and the gates: an experiment enters the paper only through `../../second-draft/kb/experiments.md`, the number that hurt is reported in the results, baselines are named, every printed number traces to a pinned artifact |
