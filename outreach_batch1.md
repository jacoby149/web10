## opener templates (one per segment, fill in brackets)

rules: never say web10/protocol/node/sovereignty/federation/records. lead
with THEIR number. the opener is ONE sentence — specific, real, can't be spam.

### right-coded opener

> "I saw your [post/video] about [specific topic] — the part about
> [specific detail] was exactly right. And I saw what happened next:
> [describe their burn in one clause, e.g. 'YouTube demonetized it under
> the same vague policy that got Lilly Gaddis hit']. That's the gap:
> [X]% of your [subscribers/followers] never even saw it."

### left-coded opener

> "Your [post/video] on [specific topic] was one of the clearest takes
> I've seen — especially [specific detail]. Then [describe burn, e.g.
> 'the algorithm buried it / YouTube flagged it / your reach dropped
> from [X] to [Y] overnight']. [X]% of your audience never saw it.
> That's the platform's decision, not your content."

### wildcard (paid-community seller) opener

> "I've been following your [community/course] on [Skool/Whop/etc.] — the
> [specific thing they do well] is exactly what [niche] needs. But you're
> still paying [X]% to [platform] and they can pull the plug tomorrow.
> What if you owned the building instead?"

### wildcard (niche creator, not paid-community) opener

> "Your [post/video] about [specific topic] — [specific detail that shows
> you actually watched/read it]. Then [describe burn: demonetized,
> shadowbanned, reach killed]. [X]% of your [subs/followers] never saw it.
> That's the platform's choice, not yours."
