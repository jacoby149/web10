## Fallback and length

If the January round rejects the paper, revise for **PVLDB's monthly research
rounds**, using the volume and rules in force then. Do not submit concurrently
while SIGMOD is considering the manuscript. Do not promise a particular PVLDB
month before the SIGMOD decision is known. If PVLDB rejects the paper, CIDR is
the next candidate under the v7 directive, subject to its then-current rules.
The SIGMOD one-year resubmission embargo must be respected for any return to
that research track.

The working plan remains **twelve pages excluding references** at both venues
(regular research or the applicable experiments/analysis category). There is no
new length or manuscript-layout task. The current official
[PVLDB submission guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html)
confirm the monthly process and applicable page allowance; re-check the
applicable volume before fallback submission rather than copying its current
calendar into a future promise.

This records strategy only. It authorizes no submission, paid experiment,
manuscript rewrite, acceptance claim or change to banked evidence.
